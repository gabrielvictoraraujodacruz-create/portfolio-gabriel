About me

From street sales to code, always solving problems

I started out selling açaí on the street at 15. After that came an artisanal açaí shop, a payroll loan company and a restaurant, where I began as a cashier and became assistant manager.

Those jobs taught me how to serve people well, organize inventory, deal with suppliers and keep daily operations running. They also showed me how much time is lost on tasks that could be simpler.

Today I bring that perspective to technology. I am at the start of my Software Engineering degree and still have a lot to learn, but I come with an advantage: I know firsthand the problems a system needs to solve.
